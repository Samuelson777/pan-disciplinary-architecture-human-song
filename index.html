Scale II

Bioacoustics & The Speech-to-Song Continuum

Song undergoes a phase shift from propositional speech via intra-syllabic f₀ plateau stabilization and isochronous inter-onset intervals (430–600ms). Classical unamplified projection relies on epilaryngeal decoupling to cluster F₃, F₄, and F₅ into a 2.4–3.4 kHz Singer's Formant (*squillo*), while high sopranos actively open the jaw to tune F₁ ≈ f₀ up to 880 Hz.

The Deutsch Speech-to-Song Illusion (2008, 2011)

Exact repetition of spoken phrases with stable intra-syllabic f₀ triggers an involuntary perceptual phase shift. Requires strict intra-syllabic pitch stability and exact inter-onset timing.

Scale III

Striatal Dopamine Processing & Stroke Rehabilitation

Musical reward dissociates temporal anticipation in the dorsal striatum (caudate nucleus) from peak consummatory pleasure in the ventral striatum (nucleus accumbens). In post-stroke non-fluent aphasia, intensive Melodic Intonation Therapy (>55h threshold) induces structural white-matter hypertrophy in the homologous right arcuate fasciculus (r-AF).

1. Mesolimbic Dopaminergic Dissociation (Salimpoor et al.)

Dorsal Striatum (Caudate) Anticipatory Phase

Encodes structural anticipation, harmonic delays, and temporal prediction prior to musical climax.

Ventral Striatum (NAc) Consummatory Phase

Mediates peak hedonic release via continuous functional coupling with STG auditory templates.

2. Melodic Intonation Therapy (MIT) Clinical Protocol

Target Patient: Severe Broca's Aphasia (2.6M–4.7M annual global cases)
Key Technique: 2-pitch minor 3rd/4th intoning + Left-hand tapping
Dosing Threshold: >55 Cumulative Hours (1.5h/day)
Structural Plasticity: Right Arcuate Fasciculus (r-AF) Hypertrophy (DTI)
Scale IV

Neural Singing Voice Synthesis (SVS) Architectural Evolution

SVS maps symbolic scores (phonemes, note pitches, durations) into high-fidelity 24 kHz audio. Generative paradigms have evolved from deterministic FastSpeech transformers to Pitch Diffusion (P-DDPM) and Flow-Matching Emformer autoencoders coupled with Differentiable Digital Signal Processing (DDSP) harmonic-plus-noise vocoders.

Generative SVS Paradigm Performance Profile

Pitch Diffusion (RMSSinger P-DDPM Mathematical Form)

Joint continuous Gaussian diffusion for f₀ and multinomial diffusion for unvoiced (UV) flags:

q(x_t | x_{t-1}) = N(x_t; √(1-β_t)x_{t-1}, β_t I)
q(y_t | y_{t-1}) = C(y_t | (1-β_t)y_{t-1} + β_t/K)

Flow-Matching + DDSP Harmonic Vocoding

Separates periodic glottal harmonic excitation from aperiodic turbulent breath noise:

x_h = F⁻¹[(1 - A)·S·F(e_h)]
x_n = F⁻¹[A·S·F(e_n)]
Output: x̂ = λ_h·x_h + λ_n·x_n
